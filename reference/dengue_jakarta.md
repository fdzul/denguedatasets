# Jakarta Dengue Cases and Weather Data from 2021 to 2024.

This data contains dengue cases hospitalized of Jakarta

## Usage

``` r
dengue_jakarta
```

## Format

A dataframe object with 2922 rows and 14 variables:

- id:

  numeric id

- reg:

  Region of Regency or City

- date:

  Date of observation

- tn:

  Minimum temperature in Celsius

- tx:

  Maximum temperature in Celsius

- tavg:

  mean temperature in Celsius

- rh_avg:

  Average relative humidity in percent

- rr:

  Rainfall in mm

- ss:

  Sunshine duration in hours

- ff_x:

  Maximum wind speed in meters by seconds)

- ddd_x:

  Wind direction at maximum wind speed

- ff_avg:

  Average wind speed in meters by second)

- ddd_car:

  Most frequent wind direction

- cases:

  Number of hospitalized patients
